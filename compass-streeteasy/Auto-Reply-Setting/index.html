StreetEasy Auto-Reply
Inbox Authorization

This system automatically sends an on-brand reply from your own Compass inbox to every StreetEasy rental inquiry — it checks your inbox about every 30 minutes, so renters hear back quickly, and every reply still comes from you. Setup has two parts in two different tools: first you create a label & filter in Gmail, then you authorize your inbox once in Make. It takes about 10 minutes.

🔒

Your password stays yours. You never share your Compass password with anyone. You enter it only on your company's own secure sign-in page — reached through Google's official sign-in flow, on your own computer — and you can remove the access yourself at any time.

Before you start
  • ✓You're signed in to your Compass Gmail on this computer.
  • ✓You have the Make automation account login (email + password), sent to you separately. This is not your Compass login.
  • ✓About 10 minutes.

How to read this guide: each screenshot is one screen, and its red numbers restart at 1. In every step, the numbers below match the red bubbles on the screenshot beside them. A "—" marks a screen you just look at or pass through (no button to click).

Part 1 of 2 · In Gmail

Create a "StreetEasy" label & filter

This makes sure the system only ever sees StreetEasy inquiries — nothing else in your inbox. You'll create the label first, then a filter that auto-applies it.

F-01Start a new label
  1. In the left sidebar, next to Labels, click the + (Create new label).
F-02Name it exactly "StreetEasy"
  1. Type the label name exactly: StreetEasy.
  2. Click Create.
F-03Open all settings
  1. Click the gear icon (top right).
  2. Click See all settings.
F-04Go to Filters and create a new one
  1. Open the Filters and Blocked Addresses tab.
  2. Click Create a new filter.
F-05Match StreetEasy mail
  1. In the From field, enter noreply@email.streeteasy.com.
  2. Click Create filter.
F-06Choose what the filter does
  1. Tick Skip the Inbox (Archive it).
  2. Tick Apply the label and choose StreetEasy.
  3. Click Create filter.
F-07Confirm the filter exists
  1. The filter now appears in the list (highlighted): Matches: from:(noreply@email.streeteasy.com) · Do this: Skip Inbox, Apply label "StreetEasy". Part 1 is done.

These inquiries now skip your inbox and sit under the StreetEasy label in the left sidebar — click it anytime to find them.

Part 2 of 2 · In Make

Authorize your Compass inbox

Log in to the Make automation account, open the scenario with your name, and connect your Compass Gmail to its two Gmail modules — the one that watches for inquiries and the one that sends the reply.

M-01Log in to Make
  1. Go to make.com/en/login and enter the email we provided.
  2. Enter the password we provided.
  3. Click Sign in.

This is the shared Make automation account — not your Compass email or password.

M-02Open Scenarios
  1. In the left sidebar, click Scenarios.
M-03Open the scenario with your name
  1. Find the scenario labeled with your own name and click to open it.
M-04The scenario you'll see
  1. This is your scenario layout: Gmail (Watch emails) → Tools → Text parser → Gmail (Send an email). The next steps connect your inbox to the two Gmail modules.
Connect your inbox to the first Gmail module (Watch emails)
M-05-1Create the connection
  1. Click the first Gmail module (Watch emails).
  2. Next to Connection, click Add — a "Create a connection" window opens.
  3. In Connection name, enter [Your Name] Gmail connection and remember it — you'll select it again later.
  4. Click Sign in with Google.
M-05-2Use another account
  1. On the Google "Choose an account" screen, click "Use another account" (don't pick the existing one).
M-05-3Enter your Compass email
  1. Type your @compass.com email.
  2. Click Next.

After Next, Compass's enterprise sign-in (Okta / 2-step) will ask you to verify it's really you. This runs on your own computer, with your own Compass account — we never see it. Once verified, you'll return to the screen in M-05-4. Your exact screen may look slightly different, but it will be a Compass-branded sign-in page.

M-05-4Confirm sign-in
  1. Click Continue.
M-05-5Grant access
  1. Tick Select all — this lets the system view and send mail for the auto-reply.
  2. Click Continue.
M-06Confirm the connection & save
  1. Back in Make, check that the connection you just named now appears in the highlighted box.
  2. Click Save.
M-07Choose where to start
  1. Select From now on.
  2. Click Save.
Connect the same inbox to the second Gmail module (Send an email)
M-08Select your existing connection
  1. Click the last Gmail module (Send an email).
  2. In the Connection dropdown, choose the connection you named earlier.
M-09Update the connection permissions
  1. In the "Update the connection permissions" window, click Continue.
M-10-1Choose your account
  1. On Google's screen, choose your @compass.com account.
M-10-2Confirm
  1. Confirm the account and click Continue.
M-10-3Allow the added access
  1. Access is already granted — review and click Continue.
M-11Confirm the connection & save
  1. Check that the connection with your name shows in the highlighted box, then click Save.
Save the whole scenario
M-12Click the Save icon
  1. At the bottom-center, click the disk (Save) icon — it has an orange dot when there are unsaved changes.
M-13Saved successfully
  1. When "The scenario was saved." appears at the bottom-right and the disk icon turns green, the scenario is saved successfully. You're done.

Your control & security

  • —We never see or store your Compass password.
  • —The automation only ever reads and replies to mail labeled StreetEasy — it ignores everything else in your inbox.
  • —You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
